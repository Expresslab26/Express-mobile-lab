About Express Mobile Lab

Express Mobile Lab is a professional mobile phlebotomy and specimen transport service serving Portland, Oregon and Vancouver, Washington. We provide fast, reliable, and compassionate blood draw services for individuals, clinics, homebound patients, and healthcare facilities.

Our Mission

To deliver accurate, timely, and patient‑centered phlebotomy services directly to your home or facility — ensuring comfort, convenience, and exceptional care.

Why Choose Us?
  • ASCP‑certified Medical Laboratory Technician
  • Flexible scheduling — mornings, evenings, weekends
  • HIPAA‑compliant handling and transport
  • Perfect for homebound or mobility‑limited patients
  • Fast turnaround for specimen delivery

Our Services

Routine Blood Draws

Standard venipuncture for labs such as CBC, CMP, lipid panel, A1C, thyroid testing, and more. We deliver specimens directly to your preferred lab.

Specialty Tests

We handle kits from Quest, LabCorp, Providence, OHSU, and specialty labs including hormone panels, genetic tests, and advanced diagnostics.

Mobile Specimen Transport

Fast, temperature‑controlled transport for clinics, assisted living facilities, and home health agencies.

On‑Site Wellness Events

Corporate wellness screenings, biometric testing, and group blood draw events.
